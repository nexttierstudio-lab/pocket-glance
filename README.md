# Pocket Glance legal pages

Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions and data-deletion instructions for the Pocket Glance
Android app, served with GitHub Pages.

- `index.html`: landing page
- `privacy.html`: Privacy Policy
- `terms.html`: Terms & Conditions
- `delete-data.html`: how to delete your data

Each page is self-contained (inline CSS, no scripts). The fonts in `fonts/` are
Bodoni Moda and Inter, both licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/).
