Pocket Glance

Delete your data

Pocket Glance has no accounts and no servers holding your records. Your data is on your phone and, if you use backup, in your own Google Drive. You can delete all of it yourself, without sending us a request.

Pocket Glance by NextTier Studio

{{tr_note}}

01Data on your phone

Uninstall Pocket Glance, or open Android Settings → Apps → Pocket Glance → Storage → Clear storage.

This permanently deletes everything in the app on that phone. It cannot be undone. Uninstalling does not cancel a Pro subscription; cancel that in Google Play.

02Google Drive backups

  1. In the app, turn off Google Drive backup and sign out.
  2. Open Google Drive settings on the web and choose Manage apps.
  3. Next to Pocket Glance, open Options and choose Delete hidden app data, then Disconnect from Drive.

03Usage and crash data

Turn off Settings → Privacy & security → Share usage & crash data to stop collection straight away. Data already sent is tied to a random identifier, not to you, and Google deletes it automatically: analytics after 2 months at our current setting (never more than 14 months), and crash and performance data within 90 days.

04Data that is kept

  • Purchase records. Google Play keeps a record of a Pro purchase so it can be restored and refunded, and for tax records.
  • Android device backup. If it is on, a copy of the app's data may be in your Google account. Manage it in Android Settings → Google → Backup.
  • Files you exported. These stay where you saved them until you delete them.
