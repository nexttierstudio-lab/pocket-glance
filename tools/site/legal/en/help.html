01Managing your data

Delete app data

Pocket Glance has no accounts, so your data is only on your phone and in any backups you made. You can delete all of it yourself, without sending us a request. To delete it from your phone:

  • Uninstall Pocket Glance, or
  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Pocket Glance → Storage → Clear storage.

This permanently deletes the app's data on that phone and cannot be undone. Uninstalling does not cancel a Pro subscription (see Manage subscriptions). Also check:

  • Google Drive backups: delete them as shown below.
  • Android device backup: if it is on, a copy may be in your Google account. Manage it in Android Settings → Google → Backup.
  • Exported files: these stay where you saved them until you delete them.
  • Purchase records: Google Play keeps these for restores, refunds and tax records.

Delete Google Drive backups

  1. In the app, turn off Google Drive backup and sign out.
  2. Open Google Drive settings in a web browser and choose Manage apps.
  3. Next to Pocket Glance, open Options and choose Delete hidden app data.
  4. Choose Disconnect from Drive.

Manage analytics preferences

  • In the app: open Settings → Privacy & security and turn off Share usage & crash data. Collection stops straight away and unsent crash reports are deleted. Data already sent is deleted by Google on schedule: analytics after 2 months, crash data within 90 days. To ask for earlier deletion, email us.
  • On this website: choose Analytics settings at the bottom of any page.

Restore a backup

  1. Install Pocket Glance and open the backup options in the app's Settings.
  2. Sign in with the Google account you backed up to.
  3. Choose the backup to restore. If you set a backup password, you will need it; we cannot recover it.

02Subscriptions & purchases

Manage subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are managed in Google Play. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pocket Glance. From there you can cancel or change your plan. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore purchases

Pro is linked to the Google account you bought it with. On a new or reset phone, sign in to Google Play with that account and open Pocket Glance: the app checks with Google Play and unlocks Pro automatically. If it does not, make sure the Play Store is using the same account, then email us with your order number.
