Privacy Policy

Your money is your business. Pocket Glance keeps your transactions, budgets and goals on your own phone. We have no accounts and no servers that receive them, so we cannot see your finances.

01Who we are

Pocket Glance is a personal budgeting app for Android, made by NextTier Studio, an independent developer. NextTier Studio is an individual developer's publishing name, not a company, and "we" in this policy means that developer. This policy explains what the app stores, the small amount of data that can leave your device, and your choices.

Contact: nexttierstudio@gmail.com

02What stays on your phone

Everything you type into the app is saved on your phone. It is not sent to us.

This covers your transactions, amounts, notes, categories, budgets, savings goals, subscriptions, reminders and settings. A few features use your device without sending anything out:

  • Currency suggestion. The app reads your SIM or network country on the device.
  • Exchange rates. Conversion uses rates built into the app.
  • App lock. Android checks your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app never receives them.
  • Reminders. These are scheduled and shown by your device.

03Usage and crash data (optional)

To fix bugs, the app sends technical diagnostics to Google Firebase. You can turn this off at any time.

While Settings → Privacy & security → Share usage & crash data is on (the default), the app sends:

  • which screens are opened, and whether an in-app purchase took place;
  • crash and error reports;
  • performance measurements, such as start-up time;
  • device model, Android version, app version, language, and country or region derived from your IP address;
  • a random installation identifier created by Firebase, and your IP address, which Google receives when data is sent.

The app is designed so that this never includes your amounts, transactions, notes, category names or anything else you type. The identifier is random and is not linked to your name, email or Google account. The app is configured not to collect the advertising ID, and this data is not used for advertising.

Turning the setting off stops collection straight away and deletes any crash reports not yet sent.

04Backups (optional)

Backups go from your phone straight to your own Google account. They never pass through us.

  • Google Drive backup. If you turn this on, you sign in with Google and the app saves backup files in a private app folder in your Drive. The app cannot see your other Drive files. You can protect backups with a password, which stays on your device and which we cannot recover. The app shows your Google account email in Settings and keeps it on your device.
  • Android device backup. If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup. Google handles this under your account settings.

Pocket Glance's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Drive access is used only for backup and restore.

05Purchases

Pocket Glance Pro is sold through Google Play, which processes your payment. We never receive your card or bank details. The app asks Google Play whether you own Pro and keeps the answer on your device. We can see order records in our Google Play developer account (such as order number, product, price and country) to handle refunds and support.

06What we never do

  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not connect to your bank or read your SMS, contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • We do not build a profile of you.

The app's permissions are limited to notifications, internet access, restarting reminders after a reboot, biometrics for app lock, and Google Play billing.

07Who processes data

Google is the only other party involved: Firebase processes usage and crash data for us as our service provider, Google Drive stores your backups in your own account, and Google Play handles purchases. Google may process data in the United States and other countries under its own safeguards. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

We may disclose the limited information we hold if the law requires it or to protect our legal rights, and it may pass to a new owner if the app is transferred. We cannot hand over your financial records to anyone, because we do not have them.

09Security

Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox and, if you enable it, app lock. Anything the app sends travels over encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for securing your device, your Google account and any backup or export files. Exported spreadsheet files are not encrypted.

10Your rights

You hold your own data, so you can view, export, correct or delete it in the app at any time, and you can turn off usage data and backups.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California or India), you may also have the right to ask us for access, correction or deletion, to object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We process usage and crash data on the basis of our legitimate interest in a reliable app. Because that data is tied to a random identifier, we usually cannot tell which records are yours. Email us and we will help as far as we can, within the time the law allows.

11Children

Pocket Glance is designed for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
