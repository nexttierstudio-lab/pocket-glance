Pocket Glance

Privacy Policy

What Pocket Glance keeps on your phone, the limited data it sends to improve the app, and the choices you have.

Pocket Glance by NextTier Studio · Effective 22 November 2026

{{tr_note}}

01About Pocket Glance

Pocket Glance is a personal budgeting app for Android, made by NextTier Studio, the publishing name of an independent developer ("we"). This policy covers the app and this website.

02Data stored on your device

Everything you enter, including transactions, amounts, notes, categories, budgets, savings goals, subscriptions, reminders and settings, is saved on your phone. Pocket Glance has no accounts and no servers that receive this data, so we cannot see it.

03Data collected

While Settings → Privacy & security → Share usage & crash data is on (the default), the app sends us, through Firebase:

  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics): screens opened and whether an in-app purchase took place.
  • Crash data (Firebase Crashlytics): crash and error reports and performance measurements, such as start-up time.
  • Device details: device model, Android version, app version, language, and approximate country or region.
  • Identifiers: an installation ID created by Firebase, and your IP address, which Google receives when data is sent.

We use this only to fix problems and improve the app. The app is designed not to include your amounts, transactions, notes or anything else you type. It does not collect the advertising ID, and this data is not used for advertising. Google deletes analytics data after 2 months and crash data within 90 days.

This website. The site sets no cookies and loads no tracking unless you choose Allow Analytics. If you do, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Performance Monitoring record the pages you view, links and buttons you click, how far you scroll, how fast pages load, your browser and device type, language and approximate location, and set first-party cookies. If you choose Decline, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is loaded. Your light/dark, language and analytics choices are saved in your browser only.

04Third-party services

Google is the only other party involved:

  • Firebase processes the app and website data described above for us.
  • Google Drive stores your backups in your own Google account, if you turn backup on.
  • Google Play Billing handles Pro purchases. We never receive your card or bank details. We can see order records (such as order number, product, price and country) to handle refunds and support.

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. Pocket Glance's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements; Drive access is used only for backup and restore.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share data for advertising. We may disclose the limited data we hold if the law requires it.

05Backups and data deletion

Google Drive backup is optional. When it is on, the app saves backup files to a private app folder in your Drive that only Pocket Glance can use, and keeps the newest five. You can protect backups with a password, which stays on your device and which we cannot recover. If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may also include the app's data in your device backup.

You can delete the app's data on your phone and your Drive backups at any time. Step-by-step instructions are in Help & Support: Delete your data.

06Your privacy choices

  • Usage and crash data: turn it off in Settings → Privacy & security. Collection stops straight away and unsent crash reports are deleted.
  • Backups: turn Google Drive backup on or off, and delete backups, at any time.
  • This website: change your choice with Analytics settings at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing consent also deletes the related cookies and IDs.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California or India), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We process app usage and crash data on the basis of our legitimate interest in a reliable app, and website analytics on the basis of your consent. Because app data is tied to an installation ID rather than your name, we may not be able to find your records, but email us and we will help as far as we can.

Pocket Glance is meant for adults and is not directed at children. We may update this policy; the current version and its effective date are always on this page.
